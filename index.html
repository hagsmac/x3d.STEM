Why X3D.

This version is one ISO-standard X3D 4.0 file (manifold.x3d): a 513 × 513 ElevationGrid of the field at rest, which any X3D browser shows, seven GLSL shaders that bring it to life in X_ITE, and metadata that states the mathematics and the evidence behind it.
